What these counts mean

The stock table groups by workbook, tab and account, matching the account-level Streamables selector. Creator is item metadata. Low inventory means count < minimum required. The default minimum is 3; the workflow's CFG override was redacted and is unverified. Awaiting approval includes staged rows with approved=no, blank, or another non-yes value. Conflicting statuses are excluded. These counts reflect the Sheet status; they do not verify B2 files. Zero eligible items is different from every item being consumed. Stale snapshots are never shown as currently healthy. Row-state totals for other queues are not eligible media counts.

Score settings

Choose how much each view or like contributes. Whole-number weights: 0–1,000. A zero weight explicitly excludes that metric; at least one weight must be positive. Missing required metrics block planning. These weights reflect your ranking preference.

Changes reorder the current gallery without an API call and cancel an unconfirmed plan. Confirmed plans retain their original formula and scores. New page loads start with 1 point per view and 10 per like.

Review help

Previously queued or recorded removals are excluded from new plans. Number to keep applies to active posts, including prior keepers.

Groups stay within one platform and account. Similarity alone does not establish that a post should be removed. Coverage measures stored posts fingerprinted; full provider history and completed matching are not guaranteed.

Keep k of n eligible posts: n − k proposed removals. Missing required metrics or already queued posts block planning. Ranking uses lifetime totals. Highest/lowest order follows Rank by; keep/remove order is an explicit alternative.

Images and counts are collected separately. Metric reasons are in each post’s info panel. Refresh metrics queues a background check; refresh groups reads the result. Previewing does not queue links. Confirmation is required; queued links are not proof of deletion.
