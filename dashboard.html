What these counts mean

The stock table groups by workbook, tab and account, matching the account-level Streamables selector. Creator is item metadata. Low inventory means count < minimum required. The default minimum is 3; the workflow's CFG override was redacted and is unverified. Zero eligible items is different from every item being consumed. Stale snapshots are never shown as currently healthy. Row-state totals for other queues are not eligible media counts.
